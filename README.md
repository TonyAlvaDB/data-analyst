# Data Analyst Road (and some personal relief)

## Relief

Hello! My name is Anthony Alvarez, but you can call me Tony. I'm a graduated software engineer looking to prevent a machine take my work. While I was in college, I tried learning everything related with Software Engineer: Frontend, Backend, Cibersecurity, DevOps. However, after some time learning tools and theory in every of those roads, I felt a little bit... overwhelmed.

A few days ago I spoke with a friend of mine (Software Engineer living in Japan) and, as a revelation, my life crossed in front of my eyes. And I remembered that I used to love Databases class. I even got a 100 in Databases II, in which we used SQL Server, Python and R to analyze a Lego sales dataset. Then I said to myself: "What happened? What made me change the path that I used to love?". And I think the answer is clear: social networks and bad advises. 

I do not know if this is something that has happened to you, but all the information that we have at hand, kinda... confuses me. So, let's say that I was checking Tik Tok (deleted by now), and I see an video of some dude or some nice lady saying something like "QUIT LEARNING JAVA, GO IS THE NEXT THING!". Literally, in the next video some other dude (sometimes the same dude) says "O NOT LEARN GO, PYTHON IS THE NEXT THING!". And I used to watch this for HOURS. This took me from the path that I used to love to learning other tools; 3 years from that and I know nothing. You do not learn a little bit of everything if you do not constantly use it. And I use nothing of what I learned in this 3 years.
 
Following with the idea on last paragraph, AI + AI-bros is the second reason I use social media with care now (and deleted Tik Tok. That s#!t rot your brain cells). In this 3 years social media went nuts! Now I do not get confused on which tool to learn, I just get confused on if I'm gonna get a job... ever! Some people (also in Tik Tok) say that machines will never get our jobs, but then other people say that they will. I think that, mentally, It's been very hard for many of us. 

Said this, it's easy to quit. Like, I could learn carpentry, or how to work with iron... But something happened a few months ago. I got engaged! And now there is no machine, no AI-bro or new-tool-to-learn-bro that can stop me. I will achieve this, even if the world is against me. I have to say that this lady makes me the most happy dude ever. And I will give everything within my power to make that lady a happy lady. And have a happy family, doing the thing that I love the most: working with beautiful data, statistics and all of that.

## The way to achieve the goal

So, now that I decided **the path**, now I can decide on how to achieve the goals. How to learn all of that and use it  
every day.

The plan is this: **go directly to the documentation**

Of course that is not all. The plan is working in 1-1.5 hours sessions. In those sessions I will read directly from the official documentation, maybe see a video or 2 in the specific topic I'm watching that day. Then, practice a lot. 

Each week I will upload a project to github, trying to explain each topic using datasets downloaded from different portals.

There is a disclaimer I need to do here: **every word that i'll write and say will come from me, no from a machine**. I will of course use AI [somewhere in time](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nkL0dF1O53k&list=RDnkL0dF1O53k&start_radio=1). But while I'm learning I will try to keep it down. My goal is to learn, so when I use AI I know when it misses and I can make corrections.

Also, I know that one of the bests ways to hold the information for longer time is to teach. So, I'm planning to open a youtube channel and, each weak, upload a video with the things I learned that week. If you are reading this you must know (maybe you already know it), that English is not my first language. I'm from Costa Rica, so *yo hablo español*. So, I'll  do my best to translate, or maybe I'll use some AI youtube shortcut or something to have 2 languages in every video. I'm not an expert, and my goal is not to be good at editing, so videos will be recorded in the best way that I can, with the tools that I already have.

This is it! This is how I'm going to leave my call center life. Have fun, ask me anything, and if you're doing this same thing, enjoy the trip.
